What MatchSong does

MatchSong finds a song in a video you filmed and lines up the original song with it, in time with the video, in place of (or mixed with) the sound your phone recorded. Finding, previewing and editing are free. Exporting the finished video needs MatchSong Pro.

MatchSong Pro

  • Yearly includes a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. After the trial, the yearly price is charged to your Apple Account, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • Yearly and Monthly subscriptions renew automatically at the same price until you cancel. You can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks Pro for good.
  • One video is a one-time purchase that unlocks exporting one project on that iPhone. It can't be restored on another device.
