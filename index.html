Questions

Good to know.

What does MatchSong do?

It swaps your video’s recording for the original audio, perfectly in sync.

Which songs can I use?

Any song file you have (MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC and more), or a better recording of the same music, like your DJ mix. Songs from streaming apps are locked and can’t be used.

Is it free?

Finding the song, listening and editing are free. Saving the finished video needs MatchSong Pro.

Does my video leave my phone?

No. Everything happens on your phone. No account, no uploads.

What if the song played faster or slower?

MatchSong notices and matches the speed, keeping the pitch.
