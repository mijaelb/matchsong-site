How it works

Three steps, and your video sounds like the real thing.

1

Pick your video

A dance class, a recital, a gig, a party: any video you filmed while the music played.

2

Add the original

The song itself, or any better recording of the same music: your DJ mix, the mixing desk, an external microphone. MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC and more.

3

It’s in sync

MatchSong finds where the music plays and lines it up. Adjust if you like, then export. The picture stays untouched, in its original quality.

Hears through the noise

Talking, cheering, a busy room: MatchSong finds the song underneath, even with the crowd louder than the music.

Skips and repeats

Paused, skipped or played again? Every part of the song is found and put in its place.

Faster or slower

Practising at 90% speed? MatchSong notices and stretches the song to match, without changing its pitch.

Your mix, your way

Keep some of the room under the song, fade in and out, set every part’s volume. Hear it all before you export.

Private by design
