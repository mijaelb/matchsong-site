How it works

Three steps, and your video sounds like the real thing.

1

Pick your video

A dance class, a recital, a party: any video you filmed while the song played from a speaker.

2

Add the original song

The song file from your iPhone: MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC and more.

3

It’s in sync

MatchSong finds where the song plays and lines it up. Adjust if you like, then export. Your picture stays untouched, in its original quality.

Made for real recordings

Precise where it counts, simple everywhere else.

To the millisecond

The song is lined up with your video to within a millisecond, measured on real recordings.

Hears through the noise

Talking, cheering, a busy room: MatchSong finds the song underneath, even when the crowd is louder than the music.

Skips and repeats

Paused, skipped or played again? Every part of the song is found and put in its place.

Faster or slower

Practising at 90% speed? MatchSong notices and stretches the song to match, without changing its pitch.

Your mix, your way

Keep some of the room under the song, fade in and out, set every part’s volume. Hear it all before you export.

Private by design
